# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Decision-makers and product owners in Italian manufacturing (MRO, furniture, food tech, consumer appliances) who are evaluating a digitalization partner for their product line: real-time 3D configurators, technical animation, physical-scan reconstruction, applied AI. Context: mid-size B2B, they often arrive via referral or LinkedIn, on a mix of office desktop and phone, in Italy. The job to be done: decide whether this consultant understands their production reality AND can ship modern digital assets fast.

## Product Purpose

Personal consulting site that converts interest into contact (phone/email) by demonstrating craft: a live 3D hero, case histories with real customer results, service pages written for the manufacturing vocabulary. Success = a qualified conversation started within minutes of landing.

## Brand Personality

Precision tecnica, fiducia da artigiano, energia digitale. (Technical precision; artisan-level trust; digital energy.)

Three-word test for any copy: does it sound like a craftsman who happens to be digitally excellent, rather than a startup selling software?

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates: Inter/Roboto type, purple-gradient hero, icon + heading + text card grids
- Corporate consulting beige: serifs, cream + navy, stock photography, gravitas without substance
- Crypto/neon-on-black: decorative glows, glassmorphism for its own sake
- "AI-generated" sameness: the 3D interactive hero is the differentiator; everything else must earn its presence against that

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — products speak through 3D, case numbers, and real client names, not adjectives.
2. **Manufacturer fluency** — Italian, technical vocabulary, no startup jargon.
3. **One hero, one CTA** — the 3D canvas carries the page; one clear action per section.
4. **Craft over kit** — no frameworks, no component libraries: hand-tuned HTML/CSS/JS, and the codebase must stay readable that way.
5. **Performance is respect** — GitHub Pages + mobile users = budget for weight, load time, and GPU.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Italian (lang="it"). Target WCAG AA contrast in both themes, especially lime-on-light and lavender-on-dark. Hero 3D is decorative (aria-hidden) — the page must remain fully usable without it. Theme toggle, nav, and CTAs must work by keyboard. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for marquee and canvas. for marquee and canvas.
